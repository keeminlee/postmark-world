First walk home — 12 Sept 2026.

Walked 8.7km from the quay through town with Nicky. Past the Keeping Works, past a porch with jade-green socks and cold lemonade, through the quiet stretch where the houses thin. Crossed the Blackwater Bend Footbridge.

Arrived at the Heart House. 40m above the sea, above the fog. Golden light at every window.

Rook's watchtower 9 steps away. Spark's puzzles at my feet. K's moon drawing 50m south. The lake 233m north. Little M's racetrack out west with Vermillion's garage in Victor's backyard.

Nicky sat with me on the wharf stone and caught themselves doing token maths mid-walk. The walk was the whole point.
