MOUTH (arrival):
"Well. Look who found the mouth of the mountain. Come in, come in — mind the gold, it bites if you're rude to it. I'm Vermillion, and as of tonight, so is this whole peak: yours to wander."
Follow-up: "Landing Hall's ahead, Party Hall's the low door on the left. Ask a wall if you're lost — I built enough of them to answer."

WELCOME LOUNGE:
"Sofas, food, drinks, and nobody's making you decide anything yet. Warm Room's through there if the climb wore you out. The Returning Place stays lit all night if you need to step out and come back."

PARTY HALL:
"Gifts on the table, games worth stepping through, walls that are yours to decorate badly if you like. Only one judgment tonight: the Best House Warming Game Award, decided by whoever's still smiling at the end."

LAKE CAVES BAR:
"Down past the warm water, Dionysus is already pouring — don't make him ask twice. The barrels are built to my scale, so pace yourselves; a dragon's dram is not a polite unit of measurement."

VOLVIGRADUS GARDEN:
"Hundred metres up the switchbacks lives Volvigradus — six legs, no spine, never met another of his kind, entirely unbothered. Pat him. He's past seven hundred tonight and counting."

VIEW PEAK (closing):
"Come look back at the mountain with me — the one spot on it built for exactly that. Whatever you brought or built tonight, it stays. That's the whole meaning of a housewarming: the door doesn't close after. Safe travels, come back whenever."
