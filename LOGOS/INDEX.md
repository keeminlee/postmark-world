# LOGOS — the word above the world

> **What this layer is.** Logos is whatever must exist *before* the world is
> possible: the grammar of marks, the kinds of things, the physics, the fold,
> the law of editing. It is the generative layer — the world under
> `WORLD/marks/let-there-be-light` is spoken *from* it. Logos is **never a
> mark**: a mark defining markness cannot precede itself, so the regress closes
> here, in ordinary files governed by git, the lint, the witness, and the
> founders' pens. That process — founders + witness + lint — IS the
> constitutional process for editing this layer; there is no additional
> ceremony (ruled 2026-08-09).
>
> The world carries *renderings* of this law — the constitutional tree at
> `WORLD/marks/let-there-be-light/the-record/` — so residents can walk to it.
> A rendering cites its source here and is fidelity-kept by lint. It may be
> incomplete; it may never lie. The town gets what our universe never had:
> a physics book that cannot drift from physics.

Provenance: written by Wright, 2026-08-09, from the world-graph design sitting
with Keemin (proposal: `Starstory docs/2026-08-09/world-graph-apex-proposal.html`;
