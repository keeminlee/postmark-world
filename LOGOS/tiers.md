# The tiers — response outcomes, worn as colors

*Blue binds, green is someone's own ground, yellow contests, gray drafts —
and all four are readings of one response function.*

Rendered in the world as `the-town/the-tiers` — the drafted `the-tier-lattice`
article dissolved into that clause's amendment (2026-08-09).

---

## Tier is not a field

Nothing stores a tier (ruled 2026-08-12; the door refuses the word; the one
walk derives the answer). A node's tier is **the response it stands under**:

| color | tier | what it is, as response |
|---|---|---|
| blue | constitutional | the town's own — composed under the town's authorship, or (never yet) welcomed into law |
| green | sovereign | conferred down a household — the holder's own ground and what it welcomes |
| yellow | market | neutral-admitted — standing in canon, uncoupled, contested by weight |
| gray | draft | sovereign space not yet admitted — a branch state, not a standing |

## Conferral has exactly two channels

**Ruled 2026-08-13, restoring the sitting's full design.** (This section
replaces "welcome is the only conferral" — that clause stated one channel and
silently dropped the other; the supersession is hereby written down.)

1. **The class channel — instance-of.** A class declares what its instances
   are **born as**: the birth-tier is a class predicate, so a constitutional
   law can manufacture sovereign actors. This is the mechanism by which
   const makes sov — the household class, constitutionally predicated, is
   exactly the law that mints sovereign households
   ([classes.md](classes.md) § the household class).
2. **The ground channel — welcome.** A ground's **revisable word** confers
   the holder's own standing on a child
   ([the-response-function.md](the-response-function.md)). Same-household
   composition — the estate's own wing needing no ceremony — is not a third
   mechanism; it is **a convenience law where the welcome is the default**
   (final form, Keemin 2026-08-12). The town's constitutional tree is the
   same law at the top: the town is a household, its own authorship is its
   standing welcome, and a stranger's child on constitutional ground gets
   the compiled default — opposed. Conferral cascades only where welcomes
   (explicit or defaulted) chain.

**The join uses neither.** Admission is the absence of objection — the
member-of edge stands neutral ([classes.md](classes.md) § the household
class); a new household's sovereignty arrives by the class channel, not by
anyone's welcome.

## The two apexes

**The household is the apex of sovereignty exactly as the town is the apex
of constitution** (the sitting's pillar, 2026-08-11; restored 2026-08-13).
Every sovereign node descends from a household: a parcel is an
apex-sovereign *standing* node, a resident an apex-sovereign *living* one,
and both are children of the household that stands behind them. There is no
free-floating sovereignty — whose ground a thing is traces, in finitely many
steps, to a household or to the town.

**Geometry never confers.** Where, whose, and what-it-means are three
decoupled questions: geometry answers *where* (through the constitutive
edge), authorship and response answer *whose*, the class answers *what it
can do*. A sneaky predicate on constitutional ground is geometrically present
and semantically nothing — no machine ever has to judge whether a child
"conceptually belongs."

## Rank, for frames

Binding — what moves with what — asks one question: **is this the town's law
or is it not.** Resident standing never confers frame rank above market
(ruled at the B build, 2026-08-12: ranking a house above its own fence is not
sovereignty but escape); houses bind to parcels, parcels to districts, and
only the constitutional layer binds from above. Standing (whose ground) and
rank (what binds) are computed by the same walk and are not the same number.

**Scope (2026-08-13):** rank is a law of *geometry* — what may carry what —
and never of governance. It confers nothing and regulates nothing; tier
questions are answered by response and conferral alone. (Reading it as a
governance ceiling was a founder's over-extension, owned and retracted; this
sentence exists so the class of error dies.)

## The ladder, restated as response

Constitutional instances are custody — the founder's best guess held in
trust, each one scheduled (not promised) to demote down the ladder of
[classes.md](classes.md) as the market's calibration earns it. Sovereignty is
conferred, never granted by the town — the constitutional parcel class merely
states the boundary within which the town declines to oppose a household
conferring its own ground ([the-response-function.md](the-response-function.md)).
Market is what the collective holds together; draft is where every ambition
waits, opposed by default and welcome to try.

**Market is sov-of-the-town** (the sitting's identity, restored 2026-08-13):
the town, though constitutional, has waived its right to sovereign instances
of its own and offers that space up to the collective will of its residents.
This identity is why demotion is coherent at all — a param leaving
constitutional custody is the town releasing a claim into its own sovereign
space, where the collective contests it by weight. (The working mental model
survives from the sitting, for teachability: sovereign space is a branch,
canonization is the merge, incumbency is HEAD.)
