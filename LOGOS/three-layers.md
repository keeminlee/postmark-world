# The three layers — the law, the log, the graph

*The word above the world; the record of what happened; the world that
follows from it.*

Rendered in the world as `the-town/the-fidelity`; the invariant abstraction as `the-town/the-invariant` (2026-08-22).
(The layers' and binding channels' 2026-08-09 renderings said other than this

---

## The layers

1. **The law** — this folder, and its renderings in the constitutional tree.
   The generative layer: classes, boundaries, the response function. Edited
   only by the constitutional process (founders + witness + lint); its
   amendments are the one write that is not a graph operation
   ([the-north-star.md](the-north-star.md) § the floor of the turtle).
2. **The log** — the two substrates of
   [state-and-time.md](state-and-time.md): every declaration ever made,
   append-only, self-witnessing. The only stored truth.
3. **The graph** — nodes, edges, standings, canon: a pure function of the
   log evaluated under the law. Derived, unwritable, recomputable by any
   clone. Projections of it ([reads-and-affordances.md](reads-and-affordances.md))
   are how anyone ever sees it.

Each layer is written by a different pen: the law by the constitutional
process, the log by everyone (through the doors and the lane, each act judged
at its gate), the graph by no one.

## Fidelity

The world carries renderings of the law so residents can walk to it. A
rendering cites its source document; the document names its renderings back
by id, on a "Rendered in the world as `…`" line; the lint holds both
directions and refuses drift. A rendering may be incomplete; it may never
lie. The town gets what our universe never had: a physics book that cannot
drift from physics — and with the class tree addressable in the world, the
book is becoming the physics.

## The binding channels

Law reaches the running town through named channels only: the engine reads
`WORLD/ENGINE.md` and the class marks; the gates run the repo's own tools;
the door's terms quote the class-nodes. Nothing binds through prose that no
machine reads — a rule that lives only in a document nobody's code consults
is a wish, and this folder tries to contain no wishes: where a clause here is
not yet consulted by the machinery, it says so, and the gap is a named entry
on the founders' cleanup list, never a silence. (That list IS the red board —
the works graph's derivation under [classes.md](classes.md)'s red
conventions; ruled 2026-08-19 — the mechanic-child convention is this
clause, mechanized.) The questions themselves are law: an **invariant** is a
standing question the town asks of itself at every fold — it reports, never
repairs, and names its method and its limits; the eight standing invariants
are classes under `postmark-invariant` in the Keeping Works (ruled
2026-08-22, the ladder audit).
