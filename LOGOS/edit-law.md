# The edit law — one primitive, three families

*To act is to declare an edge. Everything else is clothing.*

Rendered in the world as `the-town/the-edit-law` and `the-town/the-standing-children`.

---

## The one primitive

An action is **the creation of one authored edge, naming a registered edge
class**: `{seq, actor, witnesses, class, payload}`, appended to the log
([state-and-time.md](state-and-time.md)). The verb is thin — all
differentiation lives on the class named
([classes.md](classes.md)). Three families cover every act the town has ever
needed:

- **create-edges** — instantiate a node of the named class. The create-edge
  IS the authorship relation; `by:` on disk is its serialization, never an
  independent field.
- **revision-edges** — supersede: a newer declaration on your own node
  (an *amend*), a new position (a *walk* — the movement class's dials
  adjudicate it), a terminal supersession (a *withdraw*).
- **response-edges** — a word on an edge: welcomed or opposed, revisable by
  a newer word, latest wins ([the-response-function.md](the-response-function.md)).

## Every edge is from an action — no exceptions

There are no derived edges. **Every edge cites the action that brought the
relation into being** — the breath authors edges to those present; the late
walker's own *step* authors the edge to the still-standing fire. What was
once called "physics" is the set of **mandatory-edge clauses on class
contracts**, applied at action admission: your walk into earshot forms the
hearing edge whether you like it or not, because the movement class says so.
Nonconsensual, deterministic, and fully explained by the log.

## The constitutive flip

**The containment edge determines the child's coordinate frame — never the
reverse.** A child's offset has no meaning except through its declared edge,
so edge and geometry cannot disagree; there is nothing to lie about. What
remains checkable is **admissibility**: the declared footprint must fit the
declared parent (a class param, judged at the gate like every boundary).
The directory tree on disk is the edge's serialization — cache, maintained,
never truth.

## Amend

An amend is a superseding declaration on your own node — the log is never
edited; canon shows the latest admitted claim; every prior version remains
history. Geometry moved? The containment edge re-declares and the frame
follows. Children riding your frame follow per *their* words and ranks.
Backers keep or withdraw their weight in the open — a visible amendment plus
revisable responses is the whole guard against bait-and-switch; the law adds
no lock.

## Withdraw

A withdrawal is the terminal supersession: the node leaves canon; its whole
life stays in the log. Its edges dissolve with it, and its dependents resolve
by law, not judgment: **children of a withdrawn parent re-declare to the next
container, offsets rewritten so the world does not move** (an effect of the
withdrawal — the sweep's own arithmetic); the author's own predicates cascade
by default (`on_subject_withdrawal`, a class option); *others'* nodes on
withdrawn ground are never destroyed — their content is their pen. Escrow
returns; the settlement unpublishes; nothing is erased.

Relocation is withdraw-plus-create, and same-crossing declarations canonize
atomically — the promise that re-siting is free finally has its mechanism.

## Consent at the birth of an edge

A declaration may carry words of welcome for the edges it knows it will form
— a courtesy of the same act, not a fourth family. The words themselves are
ordinary response-edges and live by the response function's law.
